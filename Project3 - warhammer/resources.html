Da Mek's Workshop

Need a little extra kunnin'? These tips, tools, and tricks'll help ya get yer boyz painted faster and ready fer da next WAAAGH!

Recommended Supplies

Da Best Paints

Use acrylic miniature paints from brands like Citadel, Vallejo, Army Painter, or Pro Acryl.

Da Best Stikks

A size 1 or 2 round brush works well for most base coating and detail work.

Stuff Dat Makes Paint Stay On

Black, white, or gray primer helps paint stick to the model and makes colors easier to control.

Advice Fer New Gitz

  • Thin yer paints.
  • Don't rush da details.
  • Washes do lots o' work.
  • Finish one squad before startin' anuvver.

Good Watchin' Fer New Boyz

Watch tutorials on priming, base coating, washes, dry brushing, and edge highlights. Even da smartest Mek started somewhere.
